## 🔑 Key Concepts

### Storage Solutions

| Feature | IPFS | Arweave |
|---------|------|---------|
| **Cost** | Free (requires pinning) | One-time (~$0.01/MB) |
| **Duration** | While pinned | 200+ years |
| **Speed** | Fast | Fast |
| **Mutability** | Can update | Immutable |
| **Best For** | Development | Production |

### Payment Pricing

| Service | Cost | Monthly (1K) | Annual (12K) |
|---------|------|--------------|--------------|
| Sentiment Analysis | 0.001 SOL | 1 SOL | 12 SOL |
| Text Generation | 0.005 SOL | 5 SOL | 60 SOL |
| Image Classification | 0.002 SOL | 2 SOL | 24 SOL |
| Code Generation | 0.007 SOL | 7 SOL | 84 SOL |
| Price Prediction | 0.01 SOL | 10 SOL | 120 SOL |

### Technology Stack

**Frontend:** React 18.3, Vite 6.0, Tailwind CSS, Three.js
**Backend:** Node.js 18+, Express.js 4.21
**Blockchain:** Solana, @solana/web3.js, Anchor
**Storage:** IPFS, Arweave
**AI:** OpenRouter, snarkjs (ZK proofs)
**Compute:** Akash, Golem
